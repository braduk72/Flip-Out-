Who We Are

Gizmo Games is a UK-based Community Interest Company (CIC). Every penny of profit generated by our games goes directly towards funding our mission.

Our Mission

"When I was at my absolute lowest, these little creatures saved me. Now I want to do the same for as many of them as I can."

We are working to establish a cat sanctuary and free veterinary service for local residents who cannot afford care elsewhere. When you play Flip Out!, buy a deck, or support us in any way — you're helping make that happen.

🐱 Every game played. Every purchase made. Every cat helped.

Flip Out!

Flip Out! is a memory card-matching game with a twist — special power cards shake up every round with abilities like the Stopwatch, Freeze, Rocket, Tornado and more. Challenge the AI, battle through the Gauntlet, or go head-to-head with a friend online in real-time multiplayer. Collect beautiful card decks ranging from puppies and kittens to space exploration and ocean liners.

Available to play instantly at gizmogames.uk — no download required. Also available on iOS and Android.
